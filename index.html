How It Works

Team pickleball, the way the colleges play it

Matches use the coed collegiate team format — five events, each worth one team point. First team to three points wins, but all five events are usually played to maximize participation. Plan about 90 minutes per team match.

2 Girls + 2 Boys

That's all it takes to field a team. Rosters carry up to 6 players in the regular season and up to 8 in tournament play — enrolled students, grades 9–12, certified by their school. Schools may field more than one team until the Club Championships begin; from there, one team of up to 8 players through the State Finals.

Five Events Per Match

Girls Doubles, Boys Doubles, Mixed Doubles #1 and #2 — then, when it's tied 2–2, the HeartBreaker: a rotating singles shoot-out tie-breaker that is the most exciting finish in team pickleball.

Zero Conflict With School Sports

The season starts after every IHSAA winter sport begins and ends before the first spring sport starts. Your basketball, swimming, and wrestling programs never compete for athletes.

Season One · 2026–27

Twelve weeks. One champion.

  • Nov 17
    Season opensPractices begin at 16 home clubs statewide
  • Nov 21
    First match SaturdayTwo team matches every Saturday at your home club, through mid-January. Some clubs with lots of teams may choose to run a bracketed tournament the first couple of weeks to learn the ability levels of the teams.
  • Holiday Break
    Holiday TournamentTBD by the home clubs. Team pickleball individual tournament events of Girls Doubles, Boys Doubles, Mixed Doubles, and HeartBreaker Singles might be played.
  • Jan 23–30
    Regional Club ChampionshipsRegular-season results seed the bracket; Gold, Silver & Bronze will be competed for at all 16 clubs. Regional Club Championships must include at a minimum the top 8 teams from the regular season.
  • Feb 5–6
    Road to StateAll 16 Golds earn byes; the 32 Silvers and Bronzes play one play-in round to earn the right to face the 16 Golds.
  • Feb 12–13
    The Indiana State ChampionshipsDrew Brees Championship Court, The Picklr Noblesville — the first state champion in Indiana high school pickleball history will be crowned.

For Athletic Directors & Principals

Three steps to making history

Start (or name) your Pickleball Club

An existing club counts; a new one is easy and we'll help you form it. Just email us and we will gladly answer all your questions or get on a call with you. Your home facility offers practice court time, coaching help, and youth programming. They are there for you. That is your home club.

Certify a coach and your players

An adult coach certified by your school attends every match. Players are enrolled students, grades 9–12, certified by the school.

Register your school

Email us and we'll take it from there — home club assignment, schedule, and everything your team needs before the first serve the week of November 17.

Who We Are

The Indiana Interscholastic Pickleball Association

Our mission: to foster a lifelong love of pickleball among Indiana's youth — promoting physical fitness, healthy competition, sportsmanship, scholarship, and community through the fastest-growing sport in America. We believe every Indiana student deserves access to the game, regardless of income, ability, or how they go to school — and that a sport learned at 15 can keep a Hoosier healthy at 85.

The Indiana Interscholastic Pickleball Association, Ltd. was incorporated as an Indiana nonprofit corporation, with Articles of Incorporation approved by the Indiana Secretary of State on July 13, 2026. Federal recognition as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization is currently underway with the IRS.

With seven projects currently in the master plan, the first two — The Indiana High School Team Pickleball Club League and The Indiana High School Team Pickleball State Championships — will be our flagship programs. And it's not just going to be about the competition, although that is inevitable. Yes, we will name, with our Indoor Club partners around the great Hoosier state, 1st, 2nd, 3rd Team and Honorable Mention All-State players. Oh yes, there will be an Indiana All-Star Team that will have a special summer event planned that will be an opportunity of a lifetime. And of course — of course — there will be named the first ever Mr. Pickleball and Miss Pickleball in the state of Indiana. This just goes without saying.

But just as important as that, several amazing outreach programs, separate from the competition, will be included in the program. You will be amazed at how we will challenge the best and brightest in Hoosier High School Pickleball Clubs around the state to use their ingenuity and innovation to create something that has never been tried before. "Kids Playing for Kids."

Welcome aboard. We hope you enjoy the ride.
